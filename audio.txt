 Audio Flow in Your IVR System

  1. Receiving Audio (Knowlarity → Your System)

  - Knowlarity sends 16-bit PCM binary audio via WebSocket
  - Your system receives these raw audio chunks
  - Converts binary PCM to base64 format
  - Forwards to ElevenLabs AI agent for processing

  2. Processing Audio (ElevenLabs AI)

  - Receives base64 audio as user_audio_chunk
  - Transcribes speech to text automatically
  - Generates AI responses in Hindi/English
  - Returns audio responses as base64 agent_response_audio_delta

  3. Sending Audio (Your System → Knowlarity)

  - Takes ElevenLabs base64 audio output
  - Converts to raw audio format
  - Sends as playAudio command to Knowlarity with:
    - audioContentType: 'raw'
    - sampleRate: 16000
    - Audio content as raw data
